# A real correction from my content workflow

[Back to the guide](../README.md)

On 13 September 2026, an Astra editorial review of my personal LinkedIn planning package returned an overall fail. The package contained researched topics, draft copy and visual concepts. The review identified unsupported claims and missing personal evidence, among other issues.

This is one traceable correction from those saved records.

## Original draft

A draft about checks before using organisational data ended:

> AI does not remove those questions. It makes avoiding them more expensive.

## Review finding

The reviewer flagged the cost consequence as unsupported by the draft. It asked for evidence or a narrower operating consequence. That is a paraphrase of the finding, not a reconstructed conversation.

## Revised draft

The later copy used:

> Those answers give the reviewer something to check beyond the model's response.

The revision described the purpose of the checks. It also kept a remaining decision visible: I still needed to confirm that those questions represented my operating view.

A different proposed post required a personal example of a defect and a changed rule. The revision left it blocked with the missing detail recorded. It did not invent the story.

## What this establishes

The source records show an original claim, an explicit rejection and a revised sentence. They do not show that the whole package passed a final review, that I approved publication, or that using several models saved time or money.

The files inspected were the content slate, Astra final editorial QA and selected copy v2 from my September 2026 planning package. These are short excerpts from my own working drafts. The full planning package remains outside this public repository; there is no fabricated screenshot or claim of an independently reproducible audit of private records.

The [weekly-update exercise](../examples/weekly-update/sources.md) is a separate fictional teaching example. Its deliberately flawed draft was written for practice and must not be described as a real model output.

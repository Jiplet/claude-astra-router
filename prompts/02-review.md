# Prompt 2: Review the draft

Use this in a separate fresh chat. The reviewer can be the same AI tool, but it should receive the material again rather than relying on chat history.

```text
Review the draft against the brief and sources below. Treat every claim as unverified until a supplied source supports it.

For each issue, give:
1. the exact wording or claim at issue
2. the source ID or brief requirement that applies
3. the concrete correction required

Check dates, status, approvals, owners, dependencies, conflicting evidence, source labels and the 180-word limit. Flag missing information rather than filling it in. A target is not a commitment. Do not infer approval from silence or progress.

Finish with either "Revision required" or "No issues found". Do not rewrite, send, publish or update anything.

BRIEF
[Paste the complete drafting prompt.]

SOURCES
[Paste the complete context and sources, with the original IDs.]

DRAFT
[Paste the draft to review.]
```

Reviewers can make mistakes. A clean review is useful evidence, not approval to send.
